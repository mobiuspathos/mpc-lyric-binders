      How to type lyrics
      
      
        
    • New song: two blank lines. New verse: one blank line.
      • 
        
    • Title: the first line of each song.
      • 
        
    • Key (optional): a line starting with "Key" right under the title, like Key of C · Start on G.
      • 
        
    • Refrains are found on their own when a verse repeats word for word. To mark one yourself, start it with Refrain: or Chorus:. A verse that is only the word Refrain prints as a cue.
      • 
        
    • A refrain prints in full the first time (bold, with a bar) and as a one-line cue after that.
      • 
        
    • Long lines wrap on their own. Lines like Verse 2 are dropped.
      • 
        
    • Type never goes below 16 pt. A song that is too long is flagged in red. Cut a verse or split it into two songs (for example "Part 1" and "Part 2").
      • 
        
    • Your text is kept in this browser. Use Save text to keep a file for each binder.
