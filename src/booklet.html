    How it works
    • New song: two blank lines, then the title. New verse: one blank line.
    • Key (optional): a line starting with "Key" right under the title, like Key of C · Start on G.
    • Refrains: a verse that repeats word for word prints in bold every time. To mark one yourself, start it with Refrain:.
    • Reorder songs: drag the ≡ handle next to a song, or tap ▲ ▼. The contents and page numbers update. (Or cut and paste in "All the words".)
    • Did You Know? pages: a section titled Did You Know?. Each paragraph is one fact. They print after the songs and are not in the contents.
    • Type is as big as fits: 13 to 16 pt in the booklet, 16 to 20 pt on the phone. On the phone each song is kept on one page by shrinking the type to 14 pt; only if that is not enough does it carry on to a second page. A song never needs a page turn: it fits one page, or two facing pages (it then starts on a left-hand page, and a spare page may be added before it). A song too long for that is flagged with a note, and still prints with a page turn. Make PDF is never blocked.
    • The booklet is always a multiple of 4 pages, so it folds. Spare pages become "Our Favorites" pages with lines to write on.
    • Your text is kept in this browser. Use Save text to keep a copy as a file.
    • Only add words you have the right to print, such as public-domain carols.
